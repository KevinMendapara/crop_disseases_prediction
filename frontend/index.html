Early Blight

Alternaria solani

Plant Name: Tomato Match Confidence: 92% GPS Location: -

About this Disease

A fungal pathogen causing target-like brown spots on mature leaves...

How to Identify & Why it Happens

Concentric target board pattern starting on lower foliage.

Natural / Organic Prevention Tips

Rotate crops out of Solanaceae family for 3 years. Stake plants.

Biological Treatment (Natural Remedies)

Apply bio-agents like Bacillus subtilis.

Chemical Treatment (Pesticides, if needed)

Spray Mancozeb or Chlorothalonil.

Correct Mixing / Dosage: 2g per liter of water
